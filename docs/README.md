# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative project management lifecycle that moves work from initiation through planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement. Projects begin with a one-pager that defines the problem, objective, success metrics, stakeholders, initial timeline, risks, and resource needs, followed by a go/no-go decision gate.

Once approved, planning turns the initiative into a prioritized and estimated backlog with clear acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestone and release plans, and documented dependencies and risks. During execution, teams use project boards, daily or regular standups, weekly delivery syncs, milestone demos, small pull requests, and CI-supported quality checks to track progress and maintain delivery quality.

Risk and communication practices are continuous across delivery: teams maintain a risk register, provide regular stakeholder updates, and escalate blockers through defined paths from team triage to PM, Product Lead, and sponsor when needed. Release and deployment follow readiness checks, staged rollout and smoke-test practices, post-deploy verification, and rollback/incident playbooks.

After sprints, milestones, releases, or incidents, retrospectives capture what worked, what needs improvement, and concrete action items with owners and due dates. Clear responsibilities across Project Managers, Product Managers, Developers, QA/Testing, and stakeholders support transparent communication and consistent outcomes.
